## About

**WallpaperCraft** is a decorative building mod that adds **hundreds of customizable blocks** in a massive variety of colors, patterns, and materials.  
From clay, wood, glass, and bricks to vibrant patterned planks and panels, the mod brings nearly endless possibilities for creative builders.

This project is **a fork of a fork of an MIT-licensed project**, continuing the long evolution of WallpaperCraft through multiple authors and rewrites:

- **1.7.10** & **1.10.2** – Authored and released by **AaronMC**.
- **1.14** & onward – Authored and released by **Ommina**, rebuilt entirely from scratch.
- **1.14.4** – Complete rewrite, reusing *oliviaoliviasson's* original textures and item/block names.
- **1.6.1+** – Wood plank textures contributed by **toby20_**.
- **1.19.2+** – Ported by **Mysticpasta1** under the **MIT License** from the 1.18 version. :D

## Key Features
- **Massive Color Variety** – Match your builds with over a dozen hues for each block type.
- **Patterned Blocks** – Tiles, stripes, herringbone, and more.
- **Integrated Materials** – Works with vanilla building sets like glass, bricks, and wood.
- **Press Crafting System** – Easily recolor or repattern blocks with a custom press system.
